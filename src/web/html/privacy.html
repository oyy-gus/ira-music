1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what data {{BOT_NAME}} collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the bot's features:

  • Discord User ID — used to link your XP, level, and listening stats to your account.
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID — used to track server-level listening statistics and leaderboards.
  • Listening Statistics — including time listened, songs played, and command usage counts.
  • XP and Level Data — generated automatically from your listening activity.

We do not collect message content, voice audio, passwords, email addresses, or any sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Operate the XP progression and leveling system.
  • Display global and server leaderboards.
  • Provide listening statistics and analytics to users.
  • Improve bot performance and identify bugs.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in a private MongoDB database. We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. Access to the database is restricted to the bot developer only.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you interact with the bot. If you wish to have your data deleted, please contact us via our Support Server and we will process your request promptly.

6. Third-Party Services

{{BOT_NAME}} streams audio from YouTube and SoundCloud. These services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We do not share your personal data with these services.

7. Web Dashboard

When you log in to this web dashboard using Discord OAuth2, we temporarily store a session cookie containing your Discord user ID, username, and avatar URL. This cookie expires after 7 days and is used solely to display your profile on the dashboard. No data from your Discord account beyond what is shown is stored permanently.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your data at any time.
  • Opt out of data collection by removing the bot from all servers you control.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in our Support Server. Continued use of the bot after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related requests or questions, please contact us via our Support Server.
